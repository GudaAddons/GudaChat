A clean, minimalistic chat replacement for World of Warcraft. GudaChat strips away the clutter of Blizzard's default chat frame and replaces it with a modern, streamlined interface, while keeping all the functionality you need.

![GudaChat overview](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1468294/description_311ca7ae-af5b-4952-8da7-4c58efd8f860.png)

## New in 2.0

- **Themes**: Blizzard, Modern Dark and Classic. Switch instantly, no reload needed.
- **New settings window**: six clear pages with a live chat preview.
- **Whisper icon and quick reply**: see who whispered you and answer without leaving what you are doing.
- **Whisper ring**: bind one key; tap it to answer the last whisper, hold it to pick a person.
- **Hide chat text when not in use**: a fully clean screen until you need the chat.
- **Line spacing** for chat and history.
- **WoW: Forever support**, plus many fixes and a faster, lighter chat.

## Features

### Themes

Pick the look you like in **Settings > Appearance**. Every theme switches instantly, without a reload.

- **Blizzard** (default): the game's own gold text, dialog frames and tooltip style
- **Modern Dark**: flat dark panels with rounded corners and an accent colour (your class colour by default, or any colour you choose)
- **Classic**: the original GudaChat look

![Appearance settings with theme choice and live preview](https://i.imgur.com/PlhrPmC.jpeg)

### Hover-Reveal Header Bar

A compact header bar appears on hover with quick-access icons for tab switching, combat log, whispers, chat type selection, channels, history, and settings. Stays out of your way until you need it.

![Header bar with icons visible on hover](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1468294/description_b4abbd4c-c21d-4ce0-a692-1e0c8529a96e.png)

### Whisper Icon and Quick Reply

A small whisper icon shows how many people sent you unread whispers. Hover it to see who whispered you and their last message. Click a name to answer in a small reply box, or click the icon to answer the latest person. Turn it off in **Settings > Tabs & Alerts** if you don't need it.

![Whisper icon with the list of people who whispered you](https://i.imgur.com/a71UjdB.jpeg)

The reply box shows your last messages with that person. Press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** to send and keep it open, or **Esc** to close it. Drag it anywhere you like; it remembers the spot.

![Quick reply window](https://i.imgur.com/TPu4ue2.jpeg)

### Whisper Ring

Bind the **Whisper ring** key in **Key Bindings > GudaChat**. Tap the key to reply to the latest whisper at your mouse. Hold it to open a ring of the people who whispered you, move toward one, and let go to reply to them.

### Hide Chat Text When Not in Use

Optional: the chat text fades out together with the header when your mouse leaves the chat. It comes back when you hover, when you type, and for a few seconds when a new message arrives.

### Emoji Support

Over 30 built-in emojis that render inline in chat messages. Type shortcuts like `:)` `:fire:` `:skull:` or use the emoji picker button in the input bar. Adjustable emoji size.

![Chat with emojis and emoji picker open](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_460fc6da-04ba-4715-a7f0-e22d889cdc4c.png)

### Chat History

Messages persist across sessions and reloads, saved for each character. Browse, search, and filter history by channel. Copy entire conversations to clipboard with one click. Separate **Loot** and **Addon** logs, each with its own size limit.

![History viewer with search and channel tabs](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1468294/description_9aa93ca7-29a3-4956-9543-47225f5b6528.png)

### Copyable Links

URLs in chat are automatically highlighted and clickable. Click any link to open a copy popup, so there's no more retyping URLs by hand.

### Class-Colored Names & Player Levels

Player names are colored by class. Optional level indicators show difficulty-colored level brackets next to each player's name. Your own name is highlighted when someone mentions you, with an optional sound.

### Dedicated Whisper Tab

Enable a separate whisper tab to keep private messages organized. Incoming whispers trigger a blinking notification on the header icon.

### Combat Log Filtering

Quick-filter the combat log with subtabs: **My Actions**, or **What Happened to Me?** Your choice is kept when you switch tabs.

### Customizable Settings

Open settings with `/gc` or the header gear icon. Six pages:

- **Appearance**: theme, accent colour, live preview, chat font, size and line spacing, background
- **Chat**: lock position, message fading, hide text when not in use, scrollbar, timestamps, input bar position
- **Messages**: class colors, player levels, copyable links, name highlight and sound, emojis
- **Tabs & Alerts**: tab bar, whisper tab, tab blink notifications, whisper icon
- **History & Logs**: history, loot log, addon log, size limits, clear history
- **About**: version, supported game versions and commands

### Additional Details

- Custom context menu (right-click the tab name) for renaming, creating, and removing chat windows, font size, and background color
- Sleek custom scrollbar with scroll-to-bottom button
- Drag-to-move via the header bar with lockable position; the position is kept after Edit Mode
- Available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese
- Works on Retail, MoP Classic, TBC Anniversary, Classic Era and WoW: Forever

## Slash Commands

- `/gc` or `/gudachat`: open settings
